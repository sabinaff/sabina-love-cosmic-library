# Sabina Love Cosmic Library

Create a complete luxury digital books website (dark refined aesthetic, gold accents, clean modern typography, smooth sections).
- Name: [SABINA LOVE]
- Overall style: Dark, luxurious, sophisticated with soft grain texture
- Color palette: Deep charcoal/black background, warm gold accents, soft cream/white text
- Elegant serif + refined sans-serif typography
- Minimal, high-end book brand feel
Key Visual Element – The Globe
Overall Style:
•	Dark, deep-space aesthetic
•	Rich navy/black cosmic background with subtle stars and nebula textures
•	Elegant, clean typography (use a modern sans-serif for headings and a soft serif or refined sans for body)
•	Soft glowing accents and high contrast white/light text
•	Premium, immersive, and slightly mystical feel
Hero Section (most important):
•	Full-viewport height hero
•	Dramatic background: deep space with stars
•	Large, realistic 3D-style Earth globe positioned on the right side of the screen, showing the curvature of the planet. The Earth should have realistic blue oceans, white swirling clouds, and a glowing blue atmospheric rim. Part of the globe is in shadow, giving it a cinematic day/night effect. The globe should feel massive and slightly overlapping the right edge of the screen.
•	Left and center content overlaid on the dark space:
o	Large, bold, white uppercase title: SABINA LOVE
o	Elegant centered quote underneath in a lighter, refined font
•	Soft radial glow or subtle light source coming from behind/around the Earth to make the globe feel luminous
•	Top navigation bar: transparent/dark overlay with white links (Home, About, Collection, Contact) 
Technical feel:
•	High-resolution, photorealistic Earth globe (not cartoonish)
•	Subtle parallax or gentle floating animation on the globe if possible
•	Fully responsive
•	Premium spiritual / consciousness branding
Make the Earth globe the visual centerpiece of the entire homepage — it should feel powerful, beautiful, and slightly otherworldly.
- Place the globe carefully so it **never overlaps or interferes with text**
- Use it as a subtle background or side element that enhances the atmosphere
- Prefer soft opacity, gentle blur, or positioning behind/ beside content so the text remains perfectly clear and readable
- The globe should feel atmospheric and supportive, not distracting
Pages / Structure
Home
   - Hero with large elegant brand name, short poetic tagline, and two clear buttons (Explore Collection / Meet the Author)
   - Subtle globe integrated in the background or side without covering any text
   - Short “About the Author / the Heart” section
   - Featured quote
   - Call-to-action section (“Begin Your Journey”)
Collection
   - Clean grid or elegant cards of books
   - Soft gold hover effects
   - Consistent dark luxury styling
About
   - Author story section with refined layout
   - Optional subtle globe or cosmic atmosphere in the background
Contact
   - Simple elegant contact form or details
   - Matching dark + gold aesthetic
Design Rules
- Keep the entire website uniform and harmonious
- All pages share the same dark luxurious feel, gold accents, and typography
- The globe appears mainly on the Home page (and optionally very subtly on other pages) but always enhances rather than competes with content
- Smooth scrolling, refined spacing, premium feel throughout
- Fully responsive
Make the final result feel like a high-end digital books website that combines the clean luxury structure of Sabina Love with a carefully integrated, non-distracting cosmic Earth globe.
